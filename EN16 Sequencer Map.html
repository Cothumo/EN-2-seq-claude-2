Lane divisions

×2 · ×3 · ×4 · ×6 · ×8 · ×12 · ×16 · ×24 · ×32

Lane interval = division × base step. Defaults: lanes ×16, base step 1/16 (one note per bar).

Transport

Start resets every lane to step 1 · Continue resumes · Stop releases all notes · after power-up, clock alone starts playback if no Start arrives.

GATE & SWING

  • 10–20%
  • 25–40%
  • 45–60%
  • 65–80%

Gate is a percentage of each lane's interval. Brightness rises with the value.

Swing (press)

Off or 67%. Delays offbeat 8ths (offbeat 16ths at the 1/32 step). Notes on the beat stay put, and note length doesn't change. Magenta tint on the LED while swing is on.

Lanes 1–8 play scale degrees 1–8 from the root (default C4 = 60). Unison puts every lane on the root. Any incoming MIDI note-on sets the root.

RANDOM DIV

  • Off dark
  • Re-rolling dim, flash on each roll
  • HOLD steady

Switching two patterns

  1. Press on. Lanes that are on get a new division on every bar line (or the chosen interval).
  2. Turn to HOLD when you like one. It stops re-rolling.
  3. Press off. Your own pattern comes back and the held one is saved.
  4. Leave it on HOLD. Each press now swaps your pattern ↔ the held one, in time.

Divisions ×2/×4/×8/×16 come up most often, ×3/×6/×12 sometimes, ×24/×32 rarely. Lanes that are off are never changed. Turning a lane while random is on edits your own pattern, or the held one when at HOLD.
